## 1. Idea, Definition & Scope

### 1.1 Idea
Onboarding Digital Coworker is an AI assistant for INSPYR's onboarding team. It takes a new hire's start notice (from the JobDiva start email or a chat), checks that the data is complete and consistent, and recommends the paperwork path and the onboarding owner. It then prepares an approval package that a human approves before any action is taken.

### 1.2 Definition
Onboarding Digital Coworker is a system that supports INSPYR's consultant onboarding process from the moment a start is notified. It runs intake with adaptive questions, validates the recruiter-note fields, and applies business rules for client, location and contract. It assembles a labeled approval package, enforces a human approval gate, and executes the resulting actions only after approval. For the hackathon, every enterprise system it acts on is simulated.

The Coworker runs on Velocity 2.0, the mandatory platform for agent runtime, orchestration and models. To users it is one Coworker, reached through a local web app with five views: chat, simulated email inbox, approval, simulated alerts and status. The web app is the only mandatory channel, because Velocity 2.0 has no chat interface of its own. Inside, an Onboarding Orchestrator owns each case: it keeps the case state, decides which agent acts, manages timing and escalations, builds the package and enforces the approval gate. It routes work to specialized agents that share one context:
- **Must:** Intake and Validation; Policy and Case Setup; JobDiva and Workflow.
- **Should:** Access and Equipment; Candidate Follow-up; Onboarding Knowledge.
- **Could:** Culture and Belonging (IGS).

Notifications (email and Teams) are a shared tool with templates per audience, not an agent. The demo shows at least three specialized agents (Intake and Validation, Policy and Case Setup, JobDiva and Workflow) collaborating on one case under the orchestrator.

### 1.3 Description
**Current situation.** At INSPYR, gaps in a new hire's data are found only after the onboarding team already has the case.
- JobDiva start notes are optional and often arrive blank.
- Work arrangement is asked in three places and gives contradictory values.
- Paperwork depends on tacit knowledge (FLSA, state, city, client).
- Cases are created by hand and assigned by flagging emails by division.
- Equipment is requested from Chelsea Tech outside the flow.
- The payroll report is built manually and reconciled with a Google Doc.
- Process knowledge is held by a few people.
- Document onboarding takes about 3 days.

**Business problem.** Onboarding teams spend time chasing missing or inconsistent data from recruiters, picking paperwork from tacit rules, and rebuilding information by hand. The result is:
- slow document onboarding (about 3 days);
- wrong or missing forms;
- missed overhead and bill-back;
- payroll returns;
- dependence on a few people's knowledge.

**Current alternative.**
1. The recruiter submits the start in JobDiva and an automatic email goes out.
2. The onboarding team builds the case by hand, chases gaps with the recruiter, and picks the paperwork from tacit knowledge.
3. The candidate signs and onboarding reviews.
4. Payroll builds a manual report.
5. The recruiter separately requests the laptop from Chelsea Tech.

**Why now.** The hackathon has a fixed final submission on October 22, 2026, about eight days of build time after the October 14 checkpoint. October 16 is the cutoff: if the foundations are not ready, the extensions are dropped and all effort goes to the two Must journeys.

### 1.4 Scope
**In Scope:**
- Intake by email and chat with adaptive questions.
- Validation of the 7 recruiter-note fields and the consistency rules.
- Business rules that determine the paperwork (contract type, FLSA, state, city and client), the onboarding owner (client type and role) and the welcome kit (contract type).
- Access, equipment, overhead and bill-back (Should).
- Approval package and human approval gate.
- Simulated JobDiva, IT, equipment, payroll and email actions.
- Follow-up with a synthetic candidate (Should).
- Knowledge base with human validation (Should).
- Handoff to IGS with accelerated timing (Could).

**Out of Scope:**
- Recruiting inside JobDiva before the notification.
- Legal classification or compensation decisions.
- Background checks, drug screens, I-9 and E-Verify.
- Real equipment purchases or provisioning.
- Payroll processes after the handoff.
- Production JobDiva API and real data.
- Real e-signature and legal validation of documents.
- Consolidation of the company-wide talent database.
- Production rollout across all locations.
- The Coworker never writes to the real JobDiva.

**Won't v1 (deferred past v1):**
- The pilot (4 to 6 weeks: one client or contract type, real onboarding mailbox, real JobDiva integration) and the scale-up (all clients, full document verification, full IGS flow, analytics) come after the hackathon.
- Microsoft Teams as a channel is optional and only added after the demo is stable.
- Should and Could items are built only if the Must journeys are stable.

### Key risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Eight days to build and test | Build the two Must journeys end to end first; Should and Could only with a stable demo |
| Velocity 2.0 learning curve or licenses | Confirm licenses; a minimal case working on day one |
| Tacit business rules (forms, overhead, bill-back) | Validate the rules catalog with Tonya Ramirez's team; cite rule IDs |
| Recruiter emails with no standard format | Tolerate variations and ask for confirmation; propose a template |
| Extraction errors affecting payroll or classification | Mandatory human validation; no autonomous decisions |
| Exposure of visa, sponsorship or FLSA | Role-based permissions and data minimization in messages |
| Too many reminders to candidates or leaders | Configurable cadence and a send limit |
| Model cost overrun | Small models for extraction; usage logged per run |
| Low adoption by the teams | Co-design with onboarding and LDC |
| Demo failure on presentation day | Preloaded scenarios and a backup video |
| JobDiva API credentials not available in time | JobDiva mock as the default mode |
