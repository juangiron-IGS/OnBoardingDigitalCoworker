## Executive Summary

This register documents 31 non-functional requirements across 8 quality attribute categories for the Onboarding Digital Coworker initiative. The system is a local, single-machine demo of a multi-agent onboarding assistant running on Velocity 2.0 with synthetic data and simulated enterprise systems. The final submission is October 28, 2026.

- **Weight sits on governance and demo reliability, not scale.** The Answers record gives priority High to Functional Suitability and Usability, and Medium to Reliability (High on presentation day), Maintainability and Compatibility. Flexibility and Scalability are Low.
- **Critical NFRs protect the inviolable rules.** These cover the approval gate, role-restricted data, secrets, the append-only audit log, atomic persistence and the synthetic-data boundary.
- **One quantified performance target is confirmed.** The approval package must be Ready within 5 minutes of the recruiter's last answer (NFR-PERF-001).
- **All other numeric targets are assumed defaults.** Each is marked in its Source field and listed in the Assumptions & Defaults Log.
- **Traceability.** Each NFR traces to the Strategic Intake (SI-001), the Must journeys J1 and J2, the Answers-record keys and, since version 1.1, to the functional requirements of FRS-001 v1.1 (see NFR Traceability).
- **JobDiva is read-only.** The JobDiva connection mode is resolved as Option 1: mock by default, read-only sandbox, no writes to JobDiva in any mode (NFR-SEC-007).

## 1. Performance Requirements

### NFR-PERF-001: Approval Package Turnaround
**Category**: Performance
**Priority**: Critical

#### Requirement
The approval package shall reach status Ready within 5 minutes or less after the recruiter's last answer, for every case in the test scenario.

#### Acceptance Criteria
| Metric | Target | Threshold | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Time from last answer to package status Ready | ≤ 5 minutes | None. This is an upper limit for every case, not an average | Difference between the audit-log timestamp of the last answer and the audit-log timestamp of the package status change to Ready |
| Cases meeting the limit | 100% of cases in the test scenario | 100% | Every seed scenario run end to end |

#### Conditions
- Environment: local demo on localhost against simulated services (INF-001, INF-002, INF-003) with Velocity 2.0 models (INF-004).
- Data volume: the preloaded synthetic seed scenarios.
- A production target will be set during the pilot, not in this register.

#### Verification Method
- End-to-end runs of J1 and J2 on every seed scenario, with the interval computed from the audit log for each case.

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §2.3 (target: package ready within 5 minutes); journeys J1 and J2

#### Source
Answers record key `qa.performance-efficiency` (Technical Validations TVC-007 accepted by the user; acceptance criterion AC5, Must); SI-001 §2.3.

**Priority**: Medium

#### Conditions

- Network: localhost.
- Model-dependent step durations are governed by NFR-PERF-001, not by this NFR.

#### Verification Method

#### Related Requirements

### NFR-PERF-003: Concurrent Cases and Growth Path
**Category**: Performance
**Priority**: Medium

#### Requirement
The system shall handle several open cases at once, each isolated by case ID with no shared state between cases, and shall reach pilot and scale-up volumes by configuration and connector changes rather than redesign.

#### Acceptance Criteria
| Metric | Target | Threshold |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Simultaneously open cases | ≥ 10 | NFR-PERF-001 still met for every case |
| State shared between cases | None. Every record is keyed by case ID | Zero cross-case reads or writes |
| Scaling mode | Manual, planned scaling is acceptable at pilot stage | No automatic scaling required |

#### Conditions
- Single local machine and synthetic data. No regional or global growth is required. Variation by state, city and client is handled by rules in the catalog, not by infrastructure (production rollout across all locations is out of scope, W-009).

#### Verification Method
- Load the seed set with at least 10 cases open, run J1 and J2 concurrently, and check isolation by case ID in the audit log.

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §4 (Won't v1: pilot and scale-up); INF-002

#### Source
Answers record key `qa.scalability` (manual scaling stated as "assumed default", carried forward here with that flag; growth path from Phase 1 `scope.wont-v1`). The figure of 10 open cases is an assumed default of this document: the record says only "several".

## 2. Security Requirements

### NFR-SEC-001: Simulated Roles and Identity Attribution
**Category**: Security
**Priority**: Medium

#### Requirement
Roles shall be simulated with a user selector. Every interaction and every audit entry shall carry the selected role. No real credentials or production authentication are used in the demo.

#### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] The selector offers at least Recruiter, Onboarding Team and Approver, plus IT when the access and equipment step is included.
- [ ] 100% of audit entries record the acting role or agent.
- [ ] Each role sees its own role-specific view.
- [ ] Enterprise authentication is out of scope for the demo and is introduced before any pilot with real data.

#### Verification Method
- Switch roles in the demo and inspect the audit log.

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §3 (stakeholders), §4 (out of scope: production API and real data); INF-001

#### Source
Answers record key `qa.usability` (roles simulated with a user selector); SI-001 §4.

### NFR-SEC-002: Role-Restricted Visa, Sponsorship and FLSA Data
**Category**: Security
**Priority**: Critical

#### Requirement
Visa, sponsorship and FLSA data shall be visible only to authorized roles, and shall be hidden from every other role in every view.

#### Acceptance Criteria
| Role | Access to visa, sponsorship, FLSA | Verification |
|------|-----------------------------------|--------------|
| Onboarding Team | Visible | Switch to this role and confirm the fields display |
| Approver (Consultant Onboarding Supervisor) | Visible | Switch to this role and confirm the fields display |
| Recruiter, IT, HR, Candidate, Payroll, LDC and any other role | Not visible in any view | Switch to each role and confirm the fields are absent or masked |

- [ ] The audit log records these fields by reference, never by value.
- [ ] They never appear in messages to leaders or in mass communications (see NFR-COMP-002).

#### Verification Method
- Explicit negative test: each unauthorized role attempts to read the restricted fields in every view.

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §5 (Key Risks); SI-001 §6 (OQ-003); INF-001, INF-002

#### Source
Answers record keys `data.list` (accepted TVC-009), `qa.usability`, `qa.security`. The authorized-role list is an assumed default from SI-001 §6 OQ-003 (pending Onboarding Team confirmation), carried forward with that flag.

### NFR-SEC-003: Approval Gate and Action Authorization
**Category**: Security
**Priority**: Critical

#### Requirement
No simulated action on any system shall run without an approval ID for the current package version, and only the JobDiva and Workflow agent shall execute actions.

#### Acceptance Criteria
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Actions executed without an approval ID for the current package version | 0 (BR-002) |
| Actions attempted against a non-current package version | 100% blocked and logged |
| Actions attempted by any agent other than the JobDiva and Workflow agent | 100% refused and logged |
| Returned package | Requires a new approval (BR-003) |
| Rejected case | Stays stopped (BR-003) |

#### Verification Method
- Automated negative tests: no action without approval, version mismatch blocked, only the JobDiva and Workflow agent writes, a rejected case stays stopped.

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §2.1 (case lifecycle and approval gate), §2.3 (zero actions without approval); journey J2; INF-003

#### Source
Answers record keys `qa.functional-suitability`, `qa.maintainability`; SI-001 §2.3.

### NFR-SEC-004: Append-Only Audit Log
**Category**: Security
**Priority**: Critical

#### Requirement
Every agent action, completed or refused, shall be written to an append-only audit log, and model usage shall be logged per run and per case.

#### Acceptance Criteria
| Field | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Each agent action entry | Action, agent, case, date, and the data and rule IDs used |
| Each approval decision entry | Also records the approver, package version and approval ID |
| Restricted data | Visa, sponsorship and FLSA logged by field reference, not by value (BR-011) |
| Immutability | Entries are never edited or deleted |
| Coverage | 100% of actions and state changes have an entry |

#### Verification Method
- Compare the audit log with the case lifecycle for every seed scenario, and attempt an edit or delete (it must be refused).

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §2.1; AC9 (Must); INF-002

#### Source
Answers record key `qa.security` (accepted TVC-008 with three clarifications); SI-001 §2.1.

### NFR-SEC-005: Secrets Handling
**Category**: Security
**Priority**: Critical

#### Requirement
Secrets such as the JobDiva API token and password shall never appear in the repository, the audit log or any other log.

#### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Credentials are held in a local .env outside the repository, and the repository ships only .env.example.
- [ ] The API token is held in memory only.
- [ ] A scan of the repository and every log produced by the test scenario finds zero secrets.

#### Verification Method
- Explicit negative test: no token in any log. A repository secret scan runs in the pull-request workflow (OPS-004).

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §4 (INT-001), §5 (CONST-003); FR-ACT-008; OPS-005; TINT-001

#### Source
Answers record keys `qa.security`, `qa.maintainability`, `ops.list`, `tint.list`; SI-001 §5 CONST-003.

### NFR-SEC-007: JobDiva Write Protection
**Category**: Security
**Priority**: Critical

#### Requirement
The Coworker shall never write to JobDiva, including the sandbox. The JobDiva connector shall be one MCP server with two modes: mock (default), which reads and writes synthetic data locally, and sandbox, which only reads from the JobDiva sandbox.

#### Acceptance Criteria
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Default mode | Mock (JOBDIVA_MODE=mock) |
| Writes to JobDiva (sandbox or production) | 0 in every mode (BR-014) |
| Sandbox mode | Read-only: a test candidate's start, its start notes and the duplicate search. Enabled only when API credentials are available |
| Write requested in sandbox mode | Refused by the connector and logged |
| Mode selection | Fixed at startup by JOBDIVA_MODE and never switched automatically at runtime, so real and synthetic data are never mixed |
| Writer in mock mode | Only the JobDiva and Workflow agent, and only with an approval ID for the current package version |
| Sandbox test candidates | Created manually by team members in the JobDiva sandbox web interface, never by the Coworker |

#### Verification Method
- Negative tests: a write requested in sandbox mode is refused and logged, and no write call reaches JobDiva in either mode. Startup test per mode.

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §4 (INT-001 and "never writes to the real JobDiva"); FR-ACT-003, FR-ACT-007; TINT-001; TC-003; INF-005

#### Source
Answers record keys `tint.list`, `qa.operational-safety`, `qa.compatibility-interoperability`. The architect resolved the JobDiva connection mode as Option 1 (read-only; BR-014 unchanged; no committee approval needed). ASOT-001 TINT-001 to TINT-003 and INC-004 are to be updated to match (see Open Item 1).

### NFR-USE-003: User Error Prevention
**Category**: Usability
**Priority**: High

#### Requirement
The design shall prevent the user errors that would corrupt a case or an approval.

## 5. Reliability Requirements

### NFR-REL-001: Durable Case, Approval and Audit Data
**Category**: Reliability
**Priority**: Critical

#### Requirement
Cases and their states, package versions, approval records and the audit log shall be persisted together in the local database and shall survive an application restart.

#### Acceptance Criteria
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Data loss on restart | 0 records across cases, states, package versions, approval records and audit log |
| Approval ID integrity after restart | Every approval ID still resolves to the package version it approved |
| Regenerable data | Only synthetic seed data may be regenerated |

#### Verification Method
- Restart test mid-case and after approval, comparing records before and after.

#### Related Requirements
- INF-002; journey J2

#### Source
Answers record key `qa.reliability`.

---

### NFR-REL-002: Atomic Action and Audit Entry
**Category**: Reliability
**Priority**: Critical

#### Requirement
Each action or state change shall be saved together with its audit log entry, so neither exists without the other.

#### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] 0 state changes without an audit entry.
- [ ] 0 audit entries for changes that were not saved.
- [ ] A fault injected between the two writes leaves neither behind.

#### Verification Method
- Fault-injection test on the write path, followed by a reconciliation of case states against the audit log.

#### Related Requirements
- NFR-SEC-004; INF-002

#### Source
Answers record key `qa.functional-suitability` (immediate-consistency operations).

---

### NFR-REL-003: Visible Failure on Dependency Loss
**Category**: Reliability
**Priority**: High

#### Requirement
When a dependency fails, the affected step shall fail visibly and the system shall rest in a safe state. Nothing shall be acted on with stale or partial data.

#### Acceptance Criteria
| Failure | Required behavior |
|---------|-------------------|
| External system unavailable | Case stays in its current state, package status becomes Blocked, an alert appears in the alerts view, the failure is written to the audit log |
| Model call fails | Retried once, then the step fails visibly |
| Notification fails | Queued and retried, never blocking the case |
| JobDiva unavailable | Mode never switches to mock automatically |
| Velocity 2.0 unavailable | Demo cannot run; backup video used (NFR-AVAIL-002) |

#### Verification Method
- Failure-injection test per row, checking case state, package status, alert and audit entry.

#### Related Requirements
- INF-003, INF-004; TINT-001

#### Source
Answers record keys `qa.reliability`, `qa.compatibility-interoperability`.

### NFR-REL-004: Partial Operation
**Category**: Reliability
**Priority**: Medium

#### Requirement
If a Should agent fails, J1 and J2 shall continue, and the failure shall be shown to the approver as an exception before the decision. A failed Must step shall never be skipped.

#### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] With the Access and Equipment agent disabled, its package lines appear as exceptions and J1 and J2 complete.
- [ ] The approver sees the exception before deciding.
- [ ] With a Must step failing, the case halts visibly instead of proceeding.

#### Verification Method
- Disable each Should agent in turn and run J1 and J2.

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §2.1 (agent priorities), §5 (ASM-004); journeys J1 and J2

#### Source
Answers record key `qa.reliability`.

---

### NFR-REL-005: Idempotent Retry of Approved Actions
**Category**: Reliability
**Priority**: High

#### Requirement
If one of the approved actions fails, the completed actions shall remain recorded and the pending actions shall be retryable under the same approval ID without duplicating anything that already ran.

#### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] 0 duplicated actions after a retry.
- [ ] Completed actions are recorded with their audit entries.
- [ ] The retry uses the same approval ID and the same package version.

#### Verification Method
- Fail the second of several approved actions, retry, and compare the resulting records with a no-failure run.

#### Related Requirements
- NFR-SEC-003; journey J2; INF-003

#### Source
Answers record key `qa.compatibility-interoperability`.

### NFR-REL-006: Approval, Version and Duplicate Consistency
**Category**: Reliability
**Priority**: Critical

#### Requirement
Consistency checks shall happen immediately and before any dependent step. The approval shall be checked against the current package version, a new package version shall be created on any change, duplicates shall be blocked and a rejected case shall stay stopped.

#### Acceptance Criteria
| Operation | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Approval check | Approval matches the current package version before any simulated action |
| Change to a package | Creates a new version and invalidates the earlier approval |
| Duplicate prevention | Same person and start date blocked at Received, checked against the case store and JobDiva |
| Rejected case | Remains stopped (BR-003) |

#### Verification Method
- Automated tests: edit after approval, submit a duplicate, and attempt any step on a rejected case.

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §2.1 (case lifecycle); BR-003, BR-004; INF-002

#### Source
Answers record key `qa.functional-suitability`.

## 6. Maintainability Requirements

#### Verification Method
- Architecture review against the component catalog (`inf.list`).

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §2.1 (agents and orchestrator); INF-003

### NFR-COMP-002: Legal Boundary and Communication Minimization
**Category**: Compliance
**Priority**: Critical

#### Requirement
The Coworker shall make no legal, FLSA, classification or compensation decisions. Visa, sponsorship and FLSA information shall never appear in messages to leaders or in mass communications.

#### Acceptance Criteria
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Automated legal, FLSA, classification or compensation resolutions | 0. Such conflicts are escalated to the Onboarding Team or HR (BR-001) |
| Notification templates containing visa, sponsorship or FLSA data for leaders or mass audiences | 0 (BR-011) |
| Decisions that always require a person | Never automatic: approving, returning or rejecting the package (Consultant Onboarding Supervisor); IT's approval of the laptop with the package; assigning the follow-up owner; HR's answer to an FLSA, classification or compensation conflict; the Onboarding Team's countersign of documents and approval of the payroll handoff; LDC's approval of the welcome kit, feedback and congratulation; validating knowledge before it is shown as confirmed; the recruiter's confirmation of an ambiguous value |
| Safe state when a check cannot be completed | Fail closed: an approval ID that cannot be verified refuses the action; an unknown role hides restricted data; a case with no applicable rule shows the line as an exception instead of a guess |

#### Verification Method
- Template review and negative tests on every audience template. Seed scenarios with an FLSA or classification conflict must escalate. One negative test per human decision confirms the system cannot complete it on its own.

#### Related Requirements
- SI-001 §3 (STK-005), §5 (CONST-004); journey J2
